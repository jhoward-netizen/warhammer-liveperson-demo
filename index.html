THE CITADEL METHOD EXPLAINED

Sigma-88 formulates paint sets according to your preferred method, whether traditional Classic layering or rapid Contrast application.

01

Contrast One-Coat Method

Over an undercoat of Grey Seer or Wraithbone, Contrast paints flow into recesses and tint surfaces in a single pass, delivering instant base and shade.

Recommended for: Army batches, Orks, Tyranid swarms
02

Classic Base, Shade, Highlight

The gold standard of Warhammer painting. High-pigment Base colours, targeted Shade recessed washes, and crisp edge highlights using Layer paints.

Recommended for: Centerpiece heroes, Space Marines, Character models
03

Underpainting & "Slapchop"

Black primer drybrushed with grey and pure white (Praxeti White), glazed with translucent Contrast paints for instant volumetric shading.

Recommended for: Texture-heavy cloth, fur, terrain, speed painting

ADEPTUS ARTIFICER ADVISORY: CAVEATS & FIELD INSTRUCTIONS

CRITICAL TECHNICAL CAVEATS FOR HOBBYISTS

1. THE TWO THIN COATS DOCTRINE

Always thin your Citadel Base and Layer paints with tap water or Contrast Medium on a wet palette. Thick coats will clog fine model details. Sigma-88 will consistently specify dilution ratios.

2. PIGMENT & CLIMATE VARIANCE

Aerosol sprays (Chaos Black, Grey Seer, Wraithbone) are sensitive to humidity (>65%) and extreme temperatures. Spray in well-ventilated, temperate conditions to prevent orange peel texture.
